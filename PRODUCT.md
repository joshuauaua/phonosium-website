# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Sound artists submitting work for consideration, visitors discovering the installation's schedule and program, and curators reviewing submissions. Visitors are frequently mobile, checking the schedule in person at the installation site (Frihamnstorget, Stockholm) — layouts and touch targets are designed mobile-first.

## Product Purpose

The site is the informational and administrative surface for the Phonosium sound installation: it publishes the program (rooms, chapters, timing), explains the installation to visitors, and collects and routes artist submissions to curators. Success is a visitor who can find the schedule in seconds on a phone, and an artist who can submit work without friction or ambiguity about what happens next.

## Positioning

The site gets out of the way of the installation itself — a clear, fast reference tool for schedule and submission, not a marketing surface competing with the work for attention.

## Brand Personality

Curatorial, restrained, third-person observational — it reads like gallery wall text, not a pitch. Numbers are always present and precise (timecodes, frequencies, room numbers). No emoji, no exclamation points, no marketing superlatives.

## Anti-references

Gradient backgrounds, glassmorphism, rounded cards with heavy drop shadows, playful bouncy animations, emoji in UI copy or icons, marketing superlatives ("amazing", "revolutionary").

## Design Principles

- Design serves the task: schedule lookup and submission, never decoration for its own sake.
- Numbers anchor the work — precise timecodes, frequencies, and room numbers over vague descriptive language.
- Color is functional: a single vermillion accent marks the one live or actionable element per view, never used as decoration.
- Typography and hairline rules carry hierarchy; elevation and shadow do not.
- Mobile-first: the schedule must be legible and tappable at arm's length outdoors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA per the existing token system (see `src/design-system/DESIGN.md` and `DESIGN.md`): AAA contrast for primary text, AA minimum for interactive/link text, 44×44px minimum touch targets, and no reliance on color alone to convey state.
